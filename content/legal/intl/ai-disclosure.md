# How we use AI

> **DRAFT.** Counsel supplies the final text (OPS-C). Version: {{version}}.

## What AI does here

- Reads your resume and a job posting to explain how well they fit.
- Suggests edits to your resume and writes cover letters and answers for you to check.
- Runs practice interviews and gives feedback.
- Answers your questions in the Assistant.

## What AI does not do

AI does not submit applications, contact employers, or decide anything for you. A fit score is not your chance of getting hired.

Your data is never sent to an AI endpoint in mainland China, as the main model or as a fallback.

## Labels on AI output

AI-written text is labelled in the product. Files you export carry a machine-readable mark that says AI helped write them.
