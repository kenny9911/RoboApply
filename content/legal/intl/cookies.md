# Cookie notice

> **DRAFT.** Counsel supplies the final text (OPS-C). Version: {{version}}.

## Cookies we need

These keep the service working and cannot be turned off:

- Your sign-in session.
- Your language and theme choices.
- Which site you are on, when you switch between our sites.
- `ra_analytics_consent`: your answer to the analytics question, so we do not ask again on every page.
- `ra_tool_visitor`: set when you use a free tool without an account. It lasts 24 hours, lets the same browser open its own result again and keeps the daily limit fair. It is not used for analytics.

## Analytics

We keep our own product analytics; we run no advertising pixels.

- `ra_anon` is an anonymous id that links your visits to each other and, if you sign up, to your account. Visitors in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland choose first: until you allow analytics, this cookie is not set.
- If you do not allow analytics, we still count page visits, but each visit stands alone: no id is stored and the counts are not linked to each other or to an account.
- Product usage events are deleted after 13 months.

## Your choices

Use the "Privacy choices" link at the bottom of any page to see the question again and change your answer. Choosing "Don't allow" removes `ra_anon`.
