# Coaching policy

> **DRAFT.** Counsel reviews the final text (OPS-C). Version: {{version}}.

This policy explains how coaching works on {{brand}}. Coaching is shown only while at least one coach is listed.

## Who the coaches are

Every coach on the list is a real person who agreed to be listed. The name, photo, description, languages, topics and prices come from the coach. {{brand}} does not employ the coaches and does not write their profiles for them. Ratings are shown only after real sessions, with the number of ratings.

## How booking works

- **Booking page.** Some coaches use their own scheduling page. You book there, under that service's terms.
- **Request by email.** For other coaches, the form sends your request to the coach and to our coaching team. Only what you type in the form is sent. Your resume and account details are not shared. The coach replies to you to agree on a time and price.

## Payment

You pay the coach directly, in the way you agree with them. {{brand}} takes no payment for coaching and does not charge a fee for introductions. Prices shown are set by the coach and may change; the price you agree with the coach applies.

## What coaching can and cannot do

Coaches share advice and practice with you. There is no guarantee of interviews, offers or any other outcome.

## Problems with a coach

Write to {{support_email}}. We may remove a coach from the list. Disputes about payment are between you and the coach, but tell us so we can act on the listing.
