> **DRAFT.** Required headings only. Counsel supplies the final text (OPS-C). Version: {{version}}.

## What you get

When a friend creates a new {{brand}} account with your invite link, confirms it (email, Google or LINE) and finishes setting up, you and your friend each get 1 practice interview credit.

- You can get up to 10 invite rewards per calendar year. Your friend still gets their credit after you reach the limit.
- Credits are for practice interviews only. They have no cash value, cannot be exchanged or transferred, and are not paid out.
- We never ask you to post about {{brand}} or write a review in return for a reward.

## Who qualifies

- Only new accounts count. Existing accounts, a second account of the same person and accounts created on the same device or network as yours may not count.
- Some rewards are checked by a person before credits are added.

## How we check invites

To stop abuse we keep a short-lived, scrambled note of the browser and network used when you open or share your invite link, and when your friend signs up and uses {{brand}} in their first week. If we cannot compare the two, a person checks the invite before credits are added. We delete it after 30 days, or earlier when the account is deleted. See the privacy notice.
