Internal design guide

White canvas.
Blue actions.
Navy ink.

RoboApply helps people make a considered next move. Desk follows resume.io’s app: a white canvas with cool grey recesses, navy ink, one blue for every action, a light-blue wash for where you are, 12 px corners and navy-tinted hairline shadows. Space to explore. Evidence to support a decision.

Weight does the work

Headlines differ from body only by weight (600) and tighter tracking. The optical-size axis keeps small text regular, with no extra CSS.

  • Regular 400Evidence stays close to the decision it informs.
  • Medium 500Evidence stays close to the decision it informs.
  • Semibold 600Evidence stays close to the decision it informs.

Variable, weights 200–800, optical sizes 12–96. Source Han provides Simplified and Traditional Chinese glyphs, and CJK headlines loosen their tracking. Resume document fonts remain the user’s choice. Use --font-ui and --font-display, never a literal family name.

Optical size follows font size

The same word at three sizes: sturdy and open when small, tighter and quirkier when large.

The scale

12 / 13 / 15 / 17 / 20 / 28 px, plus fluid page titles and marketing headlines. Nothing else may set a font size.

The print shadow

A hard, misregistered second ink (--print-ink) plus a faint ink echo, with no blur. It is the one decorative type treatment: the marketing hero only, never an app heading.

The eyebrow

Sentence case, 13 px, in --text-muted, led by a small highlighter swatch. Skillry’s uppercase monospace eyebrows are deliberately not copied: they measure 2.6:1 at 11 px.

08 /What makes it RoboApply

The design serves the product’s honesty. Samples are labelled, and nothing is invented.

01

Facts over decoration

Known pay, location, work mode, and source. Unknowns labeled honestly. One clear next action.

02

Confidence through evidence

Strengths and missing evidence side by side. Meaningful criteria. Human judgment remains central.

03

A recognizable employer

Real identity, benefits, and job context. Stories require an actual author and a real source.

Make the next step obvious.

The closing band is the highlighter at full size: ink type, an ink button, and 28 px corners. It reads the same in light and dark.
