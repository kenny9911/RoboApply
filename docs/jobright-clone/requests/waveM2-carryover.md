**Written at the M2 gate of the market wave, 2026-10-11.** Source: the eight handoffs `orch/handoffs-mkt/MKT-2A.md` … `MKT-2H.md`, the M2 sections of `requests/waveM1-carryover.md`, `requests/orchestrator-queue.md` and the gate's own contract check. Everything below was **not built** at the gate: it belongs to a later bundle of the market wave, the i18n pass, the orchestrator at a later gate, or the owner. What the gate applied is listed at the end for context. `waveM1-carryover.md` still applies for its M3, M4 and M5 sections and its Owner list; an entry is repeated here only when M2 changed it. `wavePAR-carryover.md` still applies for everything outside the market wave.

Nothing in M2 was checked in a browser, against a running stack, a database or a real provider. At the gate one page was opened, read-only, on the running dev stack: RoboApply `/pricing` as a visitor (it renders; both student cards print how the student price works; no account call is made for a visitor). Plans cannot be bought on that stack (the live Stripe key is refused outside production), so no call to action of a paid plan was seen. No command reached Stripe, the GoHire worker, a model or an embeddings endpoint: every Stripe object in a test is a hand-written fake, every SQL statement of the retrieval and skills areas is tested as text and parameters only.

A bundle reads the section with its own id. An entry says what to do, where, and why; "handoff" names the M2 handoff that holds the full text.
